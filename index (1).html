Our Story

Born from a
Love of Mangoes

What started as a grandmother's summer recipe in Ratnagiri became India's most beloved mango creamery. We source only the finest Alphonso mangoes, hand-picked at peak ripeness.

🌿

100% Natural Ingredients

No artificial flavors, colors, or preservatives. Ever.

🏡

Farm-to-Bowl Philosophy

Direct partnerships with 40+ mango farmers in Ratnagiri & Devgad.

👨‍🍳

Handcrafted Daily

Every dessert made fresh each morning. No shortcuts, no compromises.
